# Code of Conduct

We want Data MCP to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participate with
respect, assume good faith, discuss ideas rather than people, and make room for
contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, unwanted
sexual attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Good-faith
reports will not result in retaliation.
